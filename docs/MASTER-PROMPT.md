# INFINITO GROUP: Master Prompt (WordPress build)

## Golden rules
1. No fake claims: testimonials, awards, certifications, partner/client logos, office locations, turnover, statistics, years of experience.
2. No unestablished credentials: export licence, IEC, FSSAI, organic certification, RERA, property ownership, construction projects.
3. Honest phrasing: "we help connect", "we assist with", "we are building". Avoid "leading", "best", "guaranteed".
4. Coach vertical: educational only, no medical or result promises.
5. Every business page shows "Managed by INFINITO GROUP".
6. Missing data is marked `[CLIENT TO CONFIRM: ...]`.

## Sitemap
Home, About Us, Our Businesses (DigiWeb, Organic Food Trade, Coach, Reality), Contact Us, Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, Thank You, 404.

## Design
White base, Deep Navy #0A1F44, Royal Blue #1E4FD8, Gold #C9A24B (accent only), Playfair Display + Inter. Minimal animation, mobile-first, WCAG AA.

## WordPress stack
Lightweight block theme, theme.json global styles, custom post type `business`, form plugin with stored entries and SMTP, Rank Math/Yoast, caching, security, backups, GDPR cookie consent.
